# Checklist (pass or fix, before you hand anything over)

1. **Story:** every important noun, action and claim of the script is on screen, in order, with the meaning unchanged.
2. **Style:** shapes, edges, shading, texture, motion and camera all follow the style file. Show it to someone with no context: could they name the style from one frame?
3. **First frame:** works as a poster. The subject is visible, big and clear; nothing is blank or still loading.
4. **Every beat earns its place:** it explains, shows an action or lands a joke. No filler loops.
5. **Continuity:** the same character, prop, colour and light from beat to beat.
6. **Readable on a phone:** text at least 28 px tall at 1080 wide, strong contrast, nothing important within 80 px of an edge, words out of the top 240 px and bottom 350 px in 9:16.
7. **Motion:** eased starts and stops, follow-through, holds long enough to read (0.5 s plus 0.3 s per word). Nothing jitters by accident.
8. **Deterministic:** scrubbing backwards and jumping to any time give the same frames as playing.
9. **Audio:** the user's audio is complete and in sync; nothing invented.
10. **Export:** the video file exists, plays, and has the right size, frame rate and length. Never call a still image or an untested file a finished video.

For new FounderNotes episodes, apply [explanation-first visual direction](../../podcast-scout-skill/references/explanation-first-visual-direction.md) alongside this checklist.

11. **Explanation-first:** state the exact viewer question and the visible action, mechanism, comparison or meaningful reflection each beat communicates. An attractive shot that teaches nothing is a fix.
12. **Acting and clarity:** where human behavior matters, original recurring characters have purposeful gestures, readable postures/expressions and reaction shots; settings and framing vary with the story, not randomly. Diagrams remain truthful, simple and legible. A deliberate still hold is allowed if documented and inspected.
13. **Treatment choice evidence:** for new FounderNotes videos, find 2–3 actually rendered candidate treatments using the **same audio passage** and review them with the decision log; a palette-only or still-only comparison is not a verified motion bake-off.

If something fails and you can't fix it, say exactly which item and why.
