# One-Object Showreel
*(adapted from @janustiu's tutorial)*

One small object appears in every shot, and each chapter applies a different motion technique to it. That one object is what makes it feel like a reel, not a list of effects.

## Must have
- **The object:** a single hero object (e.g. a red ball #FF3319) that appears in every shot and ends as the punctuation of the final word or idea.
- **Chapters:** each chapter is one technique, e.g. squash-and-stretch bounce, kinetic type slam, an orbiting dot-sphere, a ripple through a dot grid, or a tunnel of rings.
- **Frame overlay:** corner brackets, a chapter counter ("01 / 05") and a running timecode, all inside the safe zone.
- **Palette:** 3–4 flat colours (off-black #111116, cream #EFEBE4, red, cobalt #2A42FF).
- **Type:** extra-bold grotesque, with monospace labels.
## Motion
Snaps of 2–4 frames against short readable holds, real weight on bounces, and hard cuts on the beat.
## Sound
A beat around 128 BPM, with a sound tied to each action: a thud on each bounce, clicks per letter, a slice, ticks, a riser, and one beat of silence before the final hit.
