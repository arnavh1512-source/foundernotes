# One Continuous Line
*(adapted from @janustiu's Claude motion tutorial)*

A single pen stroke that never lifts: it starts as a line graph, then peels off and keeps drawing the next idea.

## Must have
- **One unbroken path for the whole film.** New shapes form *from* the old line unravelling, never by cutting or fading.
- **Paper:** off-white graph paper (e.g. #F4F1EA, grid #DAD5CA at 1 px).
- **Line:** one saturated ink (e.g. electric blue #2F5BFF), about 6 px, round cap, a very slight wobble, and a small glowing dot at the pen tip.
- **No fills, no shadows, no second colour.**
## Make any script work
- Numbers become a graph the line draws.
- Objects become one-stroke contour drawings.
- A turn in the story becomes the line overshooting, looping and pulling away into the next shape.
## Motion
- Steady drawing speed that accelerates only on long straight runs.
- The camera drifts to follow the pen tip.
## Sound
Light marimba or plucked bass, a pen-scratch texture that follows the drawing speed, a tick on axis marks, and a pluck when each shape completes.
## If it goes wrong
If the line breaks, every frame must still be one path: build the whole film as a single polyline revealed by length.
