# Inventor's Codex
*(adapted from @janustiu's tutorial)*

A quill draws an ORIGINAL invention on parchment, then the drawing comes to life and leaves the page. It must never copy a real historical page.

## Must have
- **Surface:** aged parchment (#E9D9B5) with stains, foxing and a soft vignette.
- **Inks:** sepia ink (#5A3A1E) with varied line weight and occasional blots, and red chalk (#A4452C) for construction lines.
- **Drawing order:** construction geometry first, then outlines, then cross-hatching for all shading. Everything wobbles slightly, as if hand-drawn.
- **Annotations:** unreadable mirror-script notes in the margins, with small side diagrams joined by leader lines.
## Motion
An unhurried hand rhythm: fast strokes with small pauses between. The machine's flight is the only fast moment. A page turn has real curl and shadow.
## Sound
Solo lute or harpsichord, quill scratching that follows every stroke, a tap on each blot, wooden creaks on gears, and a paper rustle on the page turn.
## If it goes wrong
Expect 2–3 rounds on the hatching. Restate the drawing order: construction, then outline, then hatching.
