# Risograph Cut-out Stop Motion
*(adapted from @janustiu's tutorial)*

Flat paper shapes printed in two riso inks slide, flip and stack on a desk like a handmade stop-motion short, imperfect on purpose.

## Must have
- **Paper:** off-white uncoated paper with fibres (#F3EFE6).
- **Inks:** two riso inks only (e.g. fluoro pink #FF48B0 and teal #00838A), plus their overprint mix.
- **Print texture:** visible halftone dots, ink grain, and slight misregistration (one ink offset 3 px).
- **Shapes:** soft short drop shadows and slightly ragged edges. No clean vector look.
## Motion
Animated on threes (about an 8 fps feel inside 30 fps), with tiny frame-to-frame jitter on every shape and squash on landings.
## Sound
Lo-fi toy piano, upright bass and brushed snare around 100 BPM; paper slides and rustles; a card thump on each landing; a rubber-stamp thunk.
## If it goes wrong
If it looks too clean, add more misregistration and jitter.
