# The Eccentric Philosopher — 75-second visual pilot

A provisional visual animatic based on the owner's supplied shooting script.
Ten original SVG shots test blocking, pacing, desk impact, perspective, memory,
expressive poses and a cup contacting a table before the hand withdraws.

This is not the finished four-minute film. It has no recorded dialogue, music or
sound effects. On-screen cues mix short dialogue excerpts with editorial text;
they are not timed captions from a measured performance. The new Twenty-five-second
interpretation cue is an editorial adaptation for the pilot, not a clinical claim.
Do not mark voice, lip synchronization or creative approval as passed.

The viewer-first plan is under research/ and actual_review remains pending.
Render with the existing tool:

```sh
node .claude/skills/motion/scripts/render.mjs .claude/skills/painted-character-motion/productions/philosopher/index.html --out studio-output/philosopher-visual/pilot.mp4 --draft
```

The production CI job renders the preview plus a contact sheet. Its checks verify
file decoding, frame count, aspect ratio and duration, not artistic success.
Record and approve the two dialogue performances before retiming the scene cuts
and expanding into the requested short film. Real circumstances and obstacles
are not depicted as disappearing when a character's interpretation changes.
