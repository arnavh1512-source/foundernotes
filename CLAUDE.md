# FounderNotes — YouTube video studio (v4.0)

This repo holds the skills, self-improving memory, and subagents used to research and produce god-tier animated educational YouTube videos for **Founder Notes** (`@FounderNotesin`).

## HARD STOP: viewer-first rule for EVERY new video

**NON-NEGOTIABLE:** Before writing a new script, obey `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/references/viewer-first-production-rule.md` and complete `studio-output/<slug>/research/viewer-first-plan.json` using `assets/viewer-first-plan.template.json`. First finish the sentence: **“This video is for someone struggling with ___; they’ll understand ___ and leave knowing how to ___.”** The order is viewer need → learning promise → verified evidence + what people may find interesting → opening that gives an insight within 30 seconds → distinct chapter payoffs → meaningful coherent visuals → natural narration/sound → reviewed **60–90-second pilot**. Run `python3 .claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/scripts/viewer_first_gate.py studio-output/<slug>/research/viewer-first-plan.json --stage pre-script`; **FAIL = no script**. After actual pilot review run the same command with `--stage full-production`; **FAIL = no full video**. An outsider must understand the problem, want the answer and *already learn something*. No filler to reach 8+ minutes; deepen research or rescope. Fictional characters never precede the viewer's problem. Competitor teardowns happen **only when the owner explicitly asks**. Structural gate PASS is not evidence that claims or videos were verified.

## Standing defaults for every session
- **Narration is always a male voice, and always the SAME voice: the Founder Notes narrator on voicebox.** Engine: **voicebox** (Chatterbox) with the profile `"Founder Notes Narrator"`, cloned from the SYNTHETIC reference `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/assets/voice/founder-notes-narrator-ref.wav` (owner-approved; not a real person). Scripts use `{"voice": {"engine": "voicebox", "name": "founder-notes", "chain": "studio"}}`; `narrate.py` creates the profile on a new machine. Start the server first: `bash .claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/scripts/voicebox_server.sh`. Each segment is generated in one pass (no voice switching); `--asr --retake 2` re-voices dropped words with a new seed. Fallbacks: Parler locked to `assets/voice/founder-notes-narrator.wav`, then Kokoro `am_michael`. Never replace the narrator unless the owner asks.
- **Every long-form video MUST run at least 8:00 (480+ seconds / 1,450–1,800+ spoken words at 1.0x speed, ~179 wpm)** (strict owner rule, 2026-11-08). Never speed up or slow down narration to fake length (`speed: 1.0` always). If the draft or narrated audio is under 8:00, deepen it ONLY with genuinely distinct verified mechanisms, examples and primary-source proof. If insufficient substance remains, STOP and research or rescope; never pad or repeat the lesson to reach 480s. `qc_video.py` hard-fails any video `< 480.0s`.
- **Task-based Claude model routing (Opus / Sonnet / Haiku only):** Read `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/references/model-routing.md` before delegation. Select **Opus medium** as the coordinator when available; use **Opus medium** for researcher, scriptwriter, visual director and independent reality checker; **Sonnet medium** for animator, sound engineer, editor and packager; and **Haiku medium** only for optional, supervised source-indexing or QC-log-reading. Verify the host's exact model/effort; never treat an alias as proof of a version or silently use an unsupported model. Escalate difficult work through effort and then Opus high, not an unavailable model.
- **Explanation before style (visual direction):** Read `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/references/explanation-first-visual-direction.md`. Fix the viewer's learning promise and each scene's visible explanatory action **before** searching the animation catalog. For psychology explainers, the preferred starting direction is original simple expressive illustrated characters, restrained colors, tactile backgrounds and occasional clear diagrams. Use meaningful reactions, familiar but varied settings, action-led cuts, different framing scales and intentional insight holds; avoid generic repeated gestures and decorative visual overload. Compare **2–3 real treatments of the same 20–40s spoken WAV excerpt**, logging measured audio, evidence, rights, and the clarity-based choice in `production/visual-treatment-comparison.md`, then review a separate 60–90s pilot before full video.
- **Full Style Universe (Never Roam Around One Look):** Route every video across our **17 native `.claude/skills/animation-styles/references/styles/*.md`** (`zackd-films`, `dan-koe`, `one-ball-showreel`, `one-line-graph`, `cgi-motion`, `lego`, `ascii-terminal`, `ink-in-water`, `ps2`, `risograph-cutout`, `renaissance-codex`, `comic-art`, `chalkboard`, `glow-sketch`, `pixel-art`, `pixel-dither`, `flat-vector`) PLUS our **5 external engines** (`bang-motion`, `vox-director` / `collage-director`, `huashu-art-motion`, `claude-drawing-skill`, `code-animation-prompts`). Keep `#101418` + `#F2B134` strictly for `K.endCard(S)` and channel branding—never lock an entire video into a repetitive dark-slate slideshow.
- **Self-Improving Memory Loop (`.claude/memory/`):** Before starting any video, `studio-visual-director`, `studio-animator`, and `studio-reality-checker` MUST read `.claude/memory/mistakes_and_pitfalls.md`, `.claude/memory/positive_recipes.md`, and `.claude/memory/production_ledger.json`. Compare recent work to avoid unnecessary repetition, but keep story coherence and clarity ahead of arbitrary style rotation. Record only verified defects and remedies using `python3 .claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/scripts/record_lesson.py`; give every used skill a real QC-backed review via `evaluate_skills.py` before approval.
- **Creator marketing:** Use `.claude/skills/marketing-growth/SKILL.md` for topic planning, YouTube packaging, Shorts distribution, and *observed* analytics. It links five vendored MIT marketing guides and defers to channel policy; no invented metrics, monetization promises, logins or posts. `.claude/skills/i-have-adhd/SKILL.md` is an **opt-in** response-format skill for concise actionable instructions; never assume the owner has a medical condition or automatically activate a persistent communication mode.
- **Optional Anatomy mechanism figures:** `.claude/skills/anatomy-figures/SKILL.md` specializes in original isometric SVG/WebGL explanatory machinery. Select only for a scene that benefits from a physical mechanism; search `anatomy:provider` and consult `references/anatomy-integration.md`. `scripts/anatomy_backend.py` previews new local figures to PNG only with independently installed, approved Anatomy. It does not produce narrated video or replace frame-accurate rendering, fact lock, 8-minute requirements, sound QC or the Opus/Sonnet/Haiku routing.
- **Optional Raea renderer:** For an approved TSX/React or 3D scene only, see `.claude/skills/raea-renderer/SKILL.md`; check installed CLI first with `scripts/raea_backend.py status`. It is a separately installed, canary FSL-1.1 engine, not bundled or default. Do not replace the standard narrator, fact lock, visual QC or existing scene engine. Do not install it without owner approval.
- **Editorial Humanizer pass:** The vendored MIT-licensed `.claude/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md` is available for natural spoken scripts and YouTube copy. Read `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/references/editorial-humanizer.md`. Humanize only **after research drafting, before final narration**; reality-check every edited claim, numerical detail, qualifier, source and citation. Do not alter code, JSON, SRT timing or narrator voice. For upload descriptions, preserve credits, source links, disclosures and chapter timestamps; rerun `package_lint.py`. Track actual quality in the skill evaluation ledger as `external/humanizer`.
- **Flexible synthetic character cast:** The established **male** `Founder Notes Narrator` remains the primary storyteller, but any episode may use additional **male and female Voicebox character profiles** for dialogue, skits, interviews, debates and reenactments when narratively justified. See `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/references/voice-casting.md` and example `assets/voice-cast.example.json`. Script segments choose a stable named `speaker`; use only distinct, owner-approved **original synthetic** profiles; no real-person or guest cloning. `narrate.py` assembles one speaker-annotated master WAV and timings; the script still requires final ASR, actual listening and QC. Do not force extra speakers when a solo narrator works. Female **characters** do not change the male channel narrator.
- **Mandatory Frame, Sound & Gap Checks:** Every video must pass `lint_scenes.py` (`0.25s` overlap/safe-zone check, `2.0s–6.0s` beat cadence, `100%` determinism scrub check) and `qc_video.py` (`>= 480.0s` duration check, `< 3.5%` anti-PPT fast-motion gate, zero `>= 0.3s` mid-video blank/frozen frame gaps, `-14 LUFS` loudness, and `<= 1 frame` audio-visual sync).
- **Subtitles burned into every video** (`assemble.py` default, bottom `180px` safe zone). **Backgrounds and camera worlds change only where the storyboard motivates a transition**; protect subject continuity.
- **Agency-style production contract:** All crew agents must read `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/references/agency-production-system.md`; choose narrative-specific techniques from the expanded external catalog, run a viewer-first approved 60–90s pilot before full length, and distinguish documented tools from installed/render-tested tools. **Agent-Reach is the default, first-attempt research gateway** via `.claude/skills/podcast-scout-skill/scripts/research_gateway.py` (`status`, `search`, `read`, `youtube-search`, `transcript`). The researcher must attempt it before native browsing; when it is not installed/available, obey the returned fallback instructions and identify the actual reader. Never auto-install, log in, or use paid transcription. Obscura is legacy.
- **Vendored motion craft helpers:** `.claude/skills/motion-design/SKILL.md` (Apache-2.0, from OpenDesign: beat maps, art direction, choreography, timing ranges) and `.claude/skills/huashu-art-motion/SKILL.md` (MIT: 35 art-style recipes, 9 explainer grammars, deterministic Canvas helper source, mostly in Chinese) are available for storyboarding and scene drawing. - **Stickman craft rules (docs only):** `.claude/skills/stickman-video-director/SKILL.md` (MIT, from kaomei/stickman-video-director) holds character-acting, prop-contact, motion, caption and sound rules for character-led explainers: slender figures with whole-body acting, hands that touch the prop, no main-picture stillness over 2.5 s (zooms and text reveals do not count), sound effects on key actions, no recycled layouts. Its renderer, generators and sample assets are **not** installed; do not copy its sample characters or call any paid service it names without owner approval. It never overrides the viewer-first rule, fact lock, narrator, subtitles or QC.

They are candidates to test in a pilot, never proof of what causes views, and they never override the viewer-first rule, fact lock, originality or channel rules.
