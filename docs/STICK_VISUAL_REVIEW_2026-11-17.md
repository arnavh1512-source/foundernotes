## Visual findings and changes
The restricted preview of the prior contact revision was actually displayed in the browser. At 0.0 seconds the legs crossed in the resting stance and short hand targets folded the arms into triangles. At 4.8 seconds the deeply compressed legs read awkwardly. At 10.5 seconds the stairs ended without a visible destination or completed action. Correct bone lengths had not caught these silhouette/story defects.

Neutral poses now use longer leg extension and near-extended hanging arms. Both knees face the same way, changing bend side only at a nearly straight passing pose at 6.4 seconds. The arm roots are closer to the chest; the shoulder crossbar and decorative hand color are removed. Strokes are thinner. Brace/strain pelvis positions were revised again after a displayed pose showed a raised knee merging with the pulling arm.

The composition now includes an open arch connected to the stairs and a visible path beyond it. The sequence lasts 14.5 seconds and reaches that destination with alternating planted support feet. It ends with a held arrival rather than an unfinished first step.
